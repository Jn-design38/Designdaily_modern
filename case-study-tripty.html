Tripty — Sustainable Luxury Lifestyle

Bespoke geometric letterforms, modular surface patterns, comprehensive guidelines, and e-commerce storefront for an enduring global lifestyle brand.

Client Tripty Lifestyle
Discipline Brand & Digital
Timeline 6 Weeks Fixed
Deliverables Guidelines + Web

The Strategic Challenge

Tripty needed to transition from an emerging artisanal label into a globally recognized luxury sustainable brand. The challenge was creating a visual mark that retained raw craft authenticity while radiating high-end editorial prestige across physical packaging, tactile apparel tags, and a modern digital storefront.

The Production System

Every component was constructed with mathematical discipline. The primary logomark was engineered using golden ratio arcs to ensure legible reduction to a 16px favicon as well as commanding visibility on retail architecture. The modular pattern system adapts seamlessly across cotton fabric weaves, luxury paper shopping bags, and digital interface backgrounds.
