// COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers before we shake hands.

Transparency is how we operate. Here are direct answers to the questions founders and directors ask most frequently.

A complete, comprehensive brand identity sprint generally runs between 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff to final master delivery. If your project includes a bespoke digital web flagship or custom packaging dielines, the timeline typically extends to 7 to 9 weeks to allow for rigorous prototyping and cross-device testing.

You receive an organized master drive containing:

  • Vector master files in AI, EPS, SVG, and high-resolution WebP/PNG formats.
  • Full typography hierarchy guides with open-source/licensed font recommendations.
  • Color specifications with exact Hex, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone values.
  • Social media avatar kits, banner systems, and presentation templates.
  • An interactive, interactive Brand Guidelines Manual (PDF + editable Figma file).
