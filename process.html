The Complete Process & Customer Journey

Predictable delivery begins with a clear system. Here is exactly how your engagement unfolds from initial submission to final master file delivery.

01 / STAGE Initial Trigger

Customer Submits a Request

You submit your project requirements through our structured online brief form. Share your business objectives, target audience, preferred aesthetic references, and key deadline requirements. No long meetings required to get started.

02 / STAGE Rapid Response

Design Daily Responds Within 1 Hour (Target)

We publicly describe our response window as a target of within one hour during standard business windows (Mon–Fri / 09:00–18:00 GMT+6). A principal designer reviews your brief personally rather than an automated bot or non-technical account manager.

03 / STAGE Strategic Alignment

The Team Prepares a Project Brief

We distill your goals into an unambiguous, structured creative brief. This documents exact deliverable formats, required asset dimensions, technical specifications, and key visual benchmarks.

04 / STAGE Collaboration

Customer Confirms or Adds Information

You review the structured brief, confirm priorities, supply any supplementary assets or copy direction, and give your formal approval before any work order is executed.

05 / STAGE Commitment

Work Order & Timeline Issued

A formal work order and locked delivery timeline are issued. Milestone checkpoints, feedback windows, and final handoff dates are documented with zero moving goalposts.

06 / STAGE Execution

The Project Begins: The 7-Stage Pipeline

Our dedicated design team initiates execution across our seven deliberate production phases:

  1. Market Research: Competitor analysis, friction audits, and positioning whitespace.
  2. Brainstorming: Lateral conceptual directions, moodboarding, and aesthetic divergence.
  3. Planning: Design system tokens, typographic scale, and copywriting layout matrix.
  4. Designing: Sub-pixel vector geometry, layout hierarchy, and optical balance.
  5. Draft: High-fidelity presentation proofs and recorded Loom video walkthroughs.
  6. Polish: Micro-animation calibration, WCAG 2.2 contrast compliance, and QA.
  7. Deliver: Production-ready packaging, print dielines, and master vector packages.
07 / STAGE Revision Guarantee

Up to Two Revisions Included

Every engagement standardly includes up to two rounds of revisions. Because we invest heavily in stages 1 through 4 (research, brief, and planning), our initial drafts hit the mark on the first pass, with revision rounds reserved for fine optical calibration.
